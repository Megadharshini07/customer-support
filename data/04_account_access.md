# Policy: Account Access & Security (POL-AA-04)

1. If a customer cannot log in and confirms they forgot their password, direct them to the
   self-service "Forgot Password" flow. Do not reset passwords manually over chat or phone.
2. If a customer reports their account was accessed by someone else without authorization
   (suspected compromise), immediately lock the account pending identity verification and
   escalate to the Security team. This is always an escalation, regardless of order/purchase
   history.
3. Identity verification for account recovery requires at least two of: registered email
   access, registered phone OTP, last order ID + billing zip code. A single factor is not
   sufficient.
4. Never read out a customer's full payment card number, password, or security answers over
   chat or phone, even to the verified account holder. Confirm only the last 4 digits.
5. Requests to merge two accounts or transfer purchase history between accounts must always
   be escalated to Tier 2 — a general agent cannot perform this action.
