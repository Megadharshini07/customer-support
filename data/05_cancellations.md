# Policy: Order Cancellations (POL-OC-05)

1. An order can be cancelled free of charge any time before it enters "Processing" status.
2. Once an order is in "Processing" or "Shipped" status, it cannot be cancelled. The customer
   should instead be directed to the Refunds & Returns policy (POL-RR-01) once the item
   arrives, or to Shipping Delays (POL-SD-02) if it is delayed/lost.
3. Subscription cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period unless the
   customer is within their first 14 days (a "cooling-off period"), in which case the
   subscription is cancelled immediately and a pro-rated refund is issued for the unused
   portion.
4. Pre-order cancellations are always free until 48 hours before the scheduled release/ship
   date, after which standard Order Cancellations rules (points 1-2) apply.
