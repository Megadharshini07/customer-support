# Policy: Data Privacy Requests (POL-DP-08)

1. Requests to view what personal data is stored about a customer can be acknowledged by a
   general agent, but the actual data export must be handled by the Data Privacy team within
   30 days, per regulatory requirement.
2. Requests to delete all personal data ("right to be forgotten") must always be escalated to
   the Data Privacy team. A general agent must not confirm deletion has occurred.
3. Marketing-email opt-outs are the only privacy-related request a general agent may complete
   directly and immediately, by updating the customer's communication preferences.
4. Never confirm or deny whether a specific named third party has requested a customer's data
   — refer any such question to the Data Privacy team without comment.
