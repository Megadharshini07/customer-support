# Policy: Refunds & Returns (POL-RR-01)

1. Standard items are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of delivery, provided the item
   is unused and in original packaging.
2. Perishable goods and digital downloads are non-refundable once delivered/accessed,
   except where the item was defective or materially not as described.
3. If a customer reports a defective item within 30 days, offer a free replacement OR a full
   refund, customer's choice. Do not require the customer to pay return shipping for defects.
4. If more than 30 days but less than 90 days have passed and the item is defective, escalate
   to a Tier 2 support agent for manufacturer-warranty review rather than approving a refund
   directly.
5. After 90 days, standard refund/return policy no longer applies; direct the customer to the
   manufacturer's warranty program if one exists, otherwise inform them no refund is available.
6. A refund must never be promised before the returned item (if physical) has been received
   and inspected, unless policy line 3 (defect within 30 days) applies.
