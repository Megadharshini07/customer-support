# Policy: Billing Disputes (POL-BD-03)

1. A customer disputing a single unrecognized charge under $50 may be refunded directly by
   any support agent after confirming the customer's identity (name + last 4 digits of
   payment method on file).
2. Disputes of $50 or more, or disputes involving more than one charge, must be escalated to
   the Billing Team and cannot be refunded directly by a general support agent.
3. If a customer claims they were charged after cancelling a subscription, first check the
   cancellation timestamp against the billing cycle date. If cancellation occurred before the
   renewal date, the charge is refundable directly (any amount). If cancellation occurred
   after the renewal date, the charge stands and is not refundable, but the agent should
   confirm the subscription is now fully cancelled going forward.
4. Chargebacks initiated through the customer's bank should never be also refunded by
   support directly — doing so causes a double refund. Escalate to Billing to confirm
   chargeback status first.
5. Duplicate charges (the same amount, same day, same merchant line) may be refunded
   directly for the duplicate amount only, by any agent, without escalation.
