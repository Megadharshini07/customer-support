# Policy: Promotional Codes & Discounts (POL-PC-09)

1. A promo code that failed to apply at checkout can be honored after the fact only if the
   order was placed within 24 hours of the code's expiry window and the customer provides the
   exact code text.
2. Promo codes cannot be combined with any other discount unless explicitly stated in the
   code's terms; if a customer claims two codes should have stacked, check the code terms
   before honoring — do not assume stacking is allowed.
3. Price-adjustment requests (item went on sale after purchase) are honored only within 7 days
   of the original purchase, as a one-time account credit for the difference, once per order.
4. Referral credits are issued automatically when a referred friend's first order ships; if a
   customer reports a missing referral credit more than 14 days after their friend's order
   shipped, escalate to the Loyalty team rather than issuing credit directly.
