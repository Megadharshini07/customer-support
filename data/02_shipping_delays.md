# Policy: Shipping Delays (POL-SD-02)

1. If a package's estimated delivery date has passed by 3 or fewer calendar days, ask the
   customer to wait, since minor carrier delays are common. Offer to set up a tracking alert.
2. If the estimated delivery date has passed by more than 3 days but fewer than 10 days,
   open a carrier trace request and offer the customer a $10 account credit for the
   inconvenience.
3. If more than 10 days have passed with no tracking movement, treat the package as lost.
   Offer the customer a full reshipment at no cost, or a full refund, customer's choice.
4. Expedited (paid) shipping that fails to meet its guaranteed delivery window is always
   refunded for the shipping fee only, regardless of how many days late, as soon as the
   customer reports it.
5. Weather events or customs holds that are publicly documented (e.g., a named storm) may
   extend the "reasonable delay" window in point 1 from 3 days to 7 days at agent discretion.
