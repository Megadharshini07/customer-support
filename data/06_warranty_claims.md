# Policy: Warranty Claims (POL-WC-06)

1. Standard manufacturer warranty covers manufacturing defects for 12 months from delivery.
   It does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear and tear.
2. Extended warranty (if purchased separately) covers accidental damage for up to 24 months,
   with a maximum of 2 claims per item.
3. To open a warranty claim, the customer must provide: order ID, description of the issue,
   and at least one photo showing the defect. A claim cannot be approved without a photo
   unless the defect is "does not power on," in which case a photo is not required.
4. Warranty claims for items bought from unauthorized third-party resellers are not covered
   and must be declined, with the customer directed back to the reseller.
5. If it is unclear whether a defect is a manufacturing fault or accidental damage from the
   customer's description, do not approve or deny the claim — escalate to a warranty
   specialist with the photo evidence attached.
